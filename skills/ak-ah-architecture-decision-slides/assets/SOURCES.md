# Brand assets

The AH mark was downloaded from the publicly served asset at https://static.ah.nl/ah-static/images/ah-ui-bridge-components/logo/logo-ah.svg on 2026-10-21. `ah-logo.png` is a transparent raster rendering of that SVG for reliable Office compatibility. The mark remains an Albert Heijn brand asset; this repository does not grant trademark rights.

The palette and layouts are an AH-inspired presentation design, not an official corporate PowerPoint template. Arial is referenced by name and is not redistributed.
